## Summary

NAYON Browser is developed by MD. AHOSHAN HABIB NAYON. Website: https://webtecit.com.
NAYON Browser may process browsing data locally to provide browser functionality. If optional
cloud synchronization, authentication, search suggestions, payment integrations or other

Review the Privacy Policy and individual permission settings before using optional services.

## What NAYON Browser stores (all on your device)

| Data | Purpose | Retention | How to delete |
|---|---|---|---|
| Settings (theme, search engine, toggles) | Remember your choices | Until cleared | Clear app storage / uninstall |
| Browsing history (normal tabs only) | History page, suggestions, recent sites | Until you delete it | History page or Settings → Clear browsing data |
| Bookmarks | Bookmarks page | Until you delete them | Bookmarks page |
| Downloads list (address, file name, size; cookies only while a download is unfinished) | Downloads page, resume | Until you delete the entry | Downloads page |
| Site icons (favicons) of visited sites and home shortcuts | Show icons in lists | Until history is cleared (all time) | Settings → Clear browsing data → All time |
| Open normal tabs (if "Restore tabs" is on) | Reopen tabs next time | Replaced as tabs change | Turn the setting off / close tabs |
| Cookies, site data and cache created by websites | Keep you signed in, load pages faster | Set by websites | Settings → Clear browsing data |

Private tabs are never written to history or saved tabs.

## What leaves your device

- **Websites you visit** receive your requests directly, as with any browser.
- **Your search engine** receives the words you search for. If **Search suggestions** is on (Settings → General), what you type in the address bar is also sent to that engine to show suggestions. This never happens in private tabs and can be switched off.
- **Translate** opens the page through Google Translate's website, so Google receives the page address.
- **Google Safe Browsing**: when available, Android System WebView may check addresses with Google to warn about dangerous sites.
- **Downloads** are fetched by Android's download manager.
- **Site icons**: to show sharp icons, NAYON Browser asks each site you visit (and each home shortcut) once a week for its own declared icon — the site's home page and icon files. No third-party icon service is used. Never for private tabs.
- **Google Lens**: the camera is used only while the Lens screen is open (Android asks you for camera permission first). A photo you take or choose for Lens is shared with the Google app (or opens lens.google.com) only when you use the Lens button. Lens photos are kept in the app's cache and deleted after about an hour.
- **Google account**: signing in to Google happens on Google's own web pages, like on any website. When you are signed in, NAYON asks accounts.google.com which account is signed in (sending only Google's own cookies, the same request Chrome makes) to show your name, email and photo on the home page. This stays in memory only; NAYON does not store or sync your account data.
- Nothing is sent to the developer. There are **no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs**.

All app data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

## Deleting your data

Use History, Bookmarks or Settings → Clear browsing data, or Android Settings → Apps → NAYON Browser → Storage → **Clear storage**, or uninstall the app.

## Passwords and site permissions

- Passwords you choose to save stay encrypted on your phone. NAYON does not upload, sync or
  share them. Uninstalling the app or "Delete all passwords" removes them.
- Camera, microphone and location are used only by websites you allow, only while you use them.
  NAYON itself does not record, store or send this data. Your allow/block choices are stored on
  your phone and can be reset in Settings → Site settings.

## Cards, addresses and documents

- Cards, IBANs, loyalty cards, addresses, identity documents, travel info and vehicles you save
  stay encrypted on your phone. Nothing is uploaded or synced. Card security codes (CVV) are
  never saved. A card or address is written into a website only when you tap it.
