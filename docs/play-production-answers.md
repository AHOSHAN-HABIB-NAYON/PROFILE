# Apply for production — উত্তর (২য় আবেদন)

প্রতিটি উত্তর ৩০০ অক্ষরের কম। কপি করে বসান।

## ধাপ ১ — About your closed test

**How did you recruit users for your closed test?**
```
I invited friends, family and regular visitors of my job circular website (cakricircular.com) who look for jobs. I shared the Google Play test link by WhatsApp, Facebook and email and asked them to use the app daily for 14 days and send feedback.
```

**How easy was it to recruit testers?**
Neither difficult or easy

**Describe the engagement you received from testers**
```
Testers used the app daily on many devices (Vivo, Redmi, Tecno, Infinix, Honor, iQOO): reading new circulars, opening posts from notifications, searching, sharing, viewing and downloading notice images and PDFs. This matches how real job seekers will use it.
```

**Provide a summary of the feedback and how you collected it**
```
Collected via Google Play testing feedback, WhatsApp and email. Testers rated 4-5 stars and asked for in-app image/PDF download, the Back button to close the image viewer, and links opening directly in the app. All were fixed in updates 2.7, 2.8 and 2.9.
```

## ধাপ ২ — About your app

**Who is the intended audience of your app?**
```
Adult job seekers in Bangladesh (18+), including students, fresh graduates and working people looking for government, private, bank and NGO jobs, admission and exam notices. The app is in Bangla and made for users who want quick, reliable job circular updates.
```

**Describe how your app provides value to users**
```
It gathers new job circulars from official sources in one place in simple Bangla, with post name, vacancies, salary, deadline countdown, original notice image/PDF and official apply link. Instant notifications help users never miss a deadline. Free, lightweight, no account needed.
```

**How many installs do you expect in your first year?**
10K - 100K

## ধাপ ৩ — Your production readiness

**What changes did you make based on what you learned during your closed test?**
```
Released 3 updates during testing: 2.7 verified app links so website links open directly in the app; 2.8 Back closes the image viewer first; 2.9 in-app download of notice images (gallery) and PDFs (Downloads) with a completion notification.
```

**How did you decide that your app is ready for production?**
```
After 14+ days of closed testing with 12+ active testers on many devices (Android 7+), there were no crashes, notifications arrived reliably even when the app was closed, all tester requests were fixed in 3 updates, and testers rated it 4-5 stars.
```
