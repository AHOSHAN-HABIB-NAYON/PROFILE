FAQ

Questions, answered

Is LifeTrack free to use?

Yes — create an account and start tracking in under a minute.

Does it work with bKash, Nagad and Rocket?

You can create dedicated bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank, card and cash accounts and track each balance separately. LifeTrack does not connect to or move money in these services — you stay in control.

Is my data secure?

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, sessions use secure HttpOnly cookies, and you can enable passkeys and two-step verification. All permissions are verified on the server.

Can I use it offline?

The app opens offline. Transactions created offline are clearly marked as pending and are only counted once the server confirms them — no duplicates.

Which languages and currencies are supported?

English, বাংলা and हिन्दी, with BDT ৳, USD $ and more currencies configurable by the administrator.

How do I install it on my phone?

Open LifeTrack in Chrome and choose “Install app”, or on iPhone tap Share → “Add to Home Screen”.
