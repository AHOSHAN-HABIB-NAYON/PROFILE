Summary

  • FilePilot has no account, no login, no ads and no analytics in this version.
  • There is no FilePilot server. The app does not upload, sell or share your files or personal data.
  • Everything the app reads is processed on your device.

This policy applies to the FilePilot Android app ("the app") published by MD AHOSHAN HABIB NAYON under the brand Webtecit Apps ("we"). It describes what the app actually does.

Information stored by the app

Only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Settings (theme, view, sorting, editor and preview options).
  • Favorites, bookmarks and the list of recently opened files.
  • Folders you granted through the folder picker.
  • Unsaved editor drafts, so your code isn't lost.
  • Recycle Bin records (original location and date) for 30 days; the files themselves stay in a hidden folder on the same storage.
  • Secure Folder files, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Encryption keys are protected by the Android Keystore and are never stored in plain text. Your PIN is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash.
  • A cached Storage Analyzer summary (folder sizes and file counts).

Cloud backup is disabled for the app's data.

Sharing

Files leave the app only when you choose Share or Open with, and then go to the app you select. No data is shared with us or with third parties. Websites you preview run inside the app; links to other websites open in your browser.

Deleting your data

  • Clear the recent list in Library, empty the Recycle Bin, or delete Secure Folder items in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes everything the app stored. Secure Folder files can't be recovered after that, so restore any you need first.
  • Files you created or moved on shared storage (for example in Documents/FilePilot Projects) remain until you delete them.

Security

The website preview is served inside the app without opening any network port; pages can't read your other files. Archives are checked so they can't write outside the chosen folder. Destructive actions always ask for confirmation.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data safety summary

No data is collected. No data is shared with third parties. No data is transmitted off the device by the app itself.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes, for example if advertising or in-app purchases are added, this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
