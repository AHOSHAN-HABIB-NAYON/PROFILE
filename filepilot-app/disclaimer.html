General

FilePilot is a tool. The developer is not responsible for how it is used or for data lost through file operations, cleaning, encryption or third-party apps. Keep backups of important files.

Android limitations

  • Android does not allow apps to access other apps' private data (for example Android/data on Android 11 and newer). FilePilot does not try to bypass this, and the Cleaner only shows files that are actually accessible.
  • On Android 10 and older, writing to SD cards requires adding the card through the folder picker.
  • Root features require a device that is already rooted; FilePilot does not root devices.

APK and file tools

APK inspection reads information Android exposes; not every APK can be analysed. Merging or compressing PDFs re-creates pages as images, so text in the result can't be selected; originals are not changed. Archive formats other than ZIP, TAR and GZIP open in other apps.
