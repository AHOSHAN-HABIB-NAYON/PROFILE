Summary

  • DevicePilot has no account, no login, no ads and no analytics.
  • There is no DevicePilot server. The app does not send your personal or device data to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Everything the app shows is read on your phone and stays on your phone.

This policy applies to the DevicePilot Android app ("the app") published by MD. AHOSHAN HABIB NAYON ("we"). It describes what the app actually does.

This information is processed on the device only. It is never uploaded, sold or shared.

Information the app stores

Only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Your settings (theme, temperature unit, alert choices).
  • Folders you added for big-file scanning.
  • The official device name, if you used "Check online".

Temperature history is kept in memory only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything. Cloud backup is disabled for the app.

Internet use

The app works offline. The only internet use is optional: when you tap Check online on the Device details page, the app downloads Google Play's public list of supported devices (from storage.googleapis.com) to show your phone's official name. Nothing about your phone is sent. The "Full specifications" button opens an independent website in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.

Actions that change your phone

  • Boost / Cool down ask Android to close background processes of apps. No app data is deleted.
  • Cleaner deletes files only after you select them and confirm.
  • Uninstalling always uses Android's own confirmation screen.
  • Screen brightness is changed only if you allow "Modify system settings" and turn the option on in Cool down.

Notifications and app lock

Optional alerts (temperature, storage, data use) are created on the device and are off by default. App lock uses Android's fingerprint, face or screen-lock prompt; DevicePilot never sees or stores biometric data.

Direct-download (Full) edition

The Full edition, distributed outside Google Play, also includes: an on-device VPN firewall (traffic of apps you block is discarded on the phone and is never forwarded, logged or inspected), an APK installer and APK file manager (files you pick), whole-storage junk and big-file scanning (with "All files access" you grant), App Hider, and SIM/carrier details (with the optional Phone permission). These also process data only on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data safety summary

No data is collected. No data is shared with third parties. No data is transmitted off the device by the app, apart from the optional device-list download described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
