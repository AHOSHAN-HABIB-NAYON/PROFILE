Delete your account and data

In the app: Profile → Delete my account and data. This removes your personal data, photos, location history, chat data and tokens, and signs out all devices.

If you cannot open the app, email support@webtecit.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Nirapod account". We complete deletion within 30 days.

Family owners must transfer ownership or delete the family first; children are removed by their parent.
