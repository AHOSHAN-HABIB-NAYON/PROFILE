Privacy Policy

Nirapod helps families share their location with each other. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account: email, name, optional photo, country, time zone, language. No phone number.
  • Location, battery level and arrivals/departures, only for family members who share (children always see a "Location sharing is on" notice; adults share only after they agree and can pause any time).
  • Device data needed for the service: device model, app version, notification token.
  • Support chat messages (AI may be used to answer; support staff can read the chat).

Who can see your location

Only members of your family circle. Nirapod staff, support agents and the AI assistant cannot see any location, route or visited place.

What we never do

  • No selling of data, no advertising or tracking SDKs, no hidden tracking, no reading of calls, SMS, chats or contacts, no audio recording.

How long we keep data

  • Location history: 3 days (Free), 30 days (Premium), 90 days (Family Plus).
  • Chat images: deleted 7 days after upload. Closed chats: deleted 7 days after closing (unless a legal hold applies).
  • Account data: until you delete your account.

Your rights

Download your data or delete your account in the app (Profile) or at /delete-account.
