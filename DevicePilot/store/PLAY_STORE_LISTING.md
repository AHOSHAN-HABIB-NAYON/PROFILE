## Full description (max 4000)

```
DevicePilot is a clean, private device manager for Android. See real information about your phone, keep it running smoothly and stay in control — with no account, no ads and no tracking.

DEVICE DETAILS
• Model, chipset, CPU clusters and GPU
• RAM, storage, display resolution, size and refresh rate
• Every camera: megapixels, aperture, focal length, stabilization and video
• Android version, security patch, kernel and build information
• When your phone was last turned on and how long it has been running

BATTERY HEALTH
• Health status, charge cycles and capacity
• Charging power, current, voltage and temperature
• Time to full charge and battery-saver status

BOOST & COOL DOWN
• Boost closes background apps in one tap and shows the real free memory before and after
• Cool down closes background apps, can lower screen brightness and shows your real battery temperature for two minutes

CLEANER
• See how much cache every app is holding and clear it from Android's own screen
• Find big files in folders you choose and delete only what you select

APPS
• Search, filter and sort installed apps
• See which apps run in the background and close them
• Uninstall several apps — Android always asks you to confirm

MORE
• Temperature and Android thermal status with a live chart
• Storage and network usage per app
• Optimize scan with clear, honest suggestions
• Speed-up guide for Android's animation settings
• App lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Light and dark theme

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
DevicePilot has no account, no ads and no analytics, and there is no DevicePilot server. Information is read on your phone and stays on your phone. Every value comes from official Android features — if your phone does not provide a value, DevicePilot says so instead of guessing.

Developer: MD. AHOSHAN HABIB NAYON — webtecit.com
Support: support.webtecit@gmail.com
```
